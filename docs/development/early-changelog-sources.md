There is a concrete counterexample to treating tag creation as release day: the [3609 tag was recorded on 2011-12-09](https://github.com/FAForever/fa/commit/9ec65ced1a248d848eb00d28ae3af1d46d3edf9d), while its contemporary release announcement is dated 2011-12-08. Commit author/committer times are not deployment timestamps.

No explicit public-release evidence for 3617 or 3618 was found in this reachable history. Neither the absent 3605/3606 version markers nor the withdrawn 3635 note acquire dates from neighboring versions. The publisher's surviving 2012 post index was also checked; it has a gap between March and September and does not resolve the missing dates. Twelve exact dates therefore remain unverified. The reader links to the newly found Git evidence where available while retaining the unverified-date label.

## Presentation

MediaWiki's leading-space `<pre>` convention was used for ordinary notes in 3605, 3615 and 3619, and the first importer also wrapped multiline paragraphs in 3616 as `<pre>`. These now become normal paragraphs or stat/bullet lists without rewritten wording, numbers, invented headings or descriptions. Explicit source-code blocks and blog preformatted blocks keep their code presentation. Regeneration continues to check the original source hashes.

Historical illustrations are presentation metadata in `ui/src/shared/historicalChangelogIcons.ts`. They preserve the archive's wording and block anchors while correcting presentation-only markup. Complete unit captions are matched to identities checked against the game's `LOC/US/strings_db.lua` at tag 3636; the original spelling remains visible. Faction-only captions under an explicit ACU/SCU heading identify that faction's commander, while broad captions such as "Aeon t2" receive the existing faction emblem. Ambiguous general headings keep their text presentation.

Mixed wiki markup can write a faction label as a paragraph beside real faction headings: 3626's UEF belongs beside Cybran, Seraphim and Aeon under ACUs. A shared effective heading level makes these faction sections peers in both the body and contents, preserving the original blocks and anchors. Multi-faction group labels keep their portraits in the body, but the contents does not choose one faction's portrait to represent the group.

Explicit SCU upgrade captions can use the later balance archive's faction-specific enhancement glyphs from the pinned patchnotes revision. These are existing illustrative assets, not a reconstruction of original 2012 artwork. A shared mapping supplies the body and contents navigation, with the existing 48px and 24px icon sizes. Only the 28 bundled early-note identities select these rules; modern notes and client release notes do not.

Contributor names published in a heading immediately inside a credits section are presented as ordinary body text and excluded from the contents, while the credits section label remains. The all-note caption scan also identified Atlantis and Monkeylord in 3626; their verified unit portraits and navigation entries now sit beside the general veterancy subsection rather than inside it. The original wording and block anchors remain intact.
