# Early patchnote source ledger

Seed: https://web.archive.org/web/20150128084120/http://wiki.faforever.com/index.php?title=Main_Page

33 indexed candidates; 28 additional notes; five existing notes retained. Two existing dates corrected from explicit official release headings.

Release dates below are independent of capture/edit timestamps. Unknown dates stay unknown. 3605 and 3606 remain separately classified legacy notes because the full lineage of their cumulative changes is unverified. No new official GPG/Steam release records are inferred from those ranges.

The 3629 announcement identifies Release Candidate 1. The 3635 wiki note says the patch was withdrawn and its changes folded into 3636. The 3637 announcement was posted on December 13; its December 12 release date is independently explicit in the official changelog, so the existing date is retained.

The 3608 contemporary post establishes availability by October 20, 2011, not the exact release date. Other undated wiki pages do not gain dates from their edit/capture times or their place in the version sequence.
