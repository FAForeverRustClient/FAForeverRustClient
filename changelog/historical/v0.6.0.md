Changes since v0.5.7.

## Training and community

- Read training guides and watch videos inside the Training tab. (#68)
- Recorded build orders show a build timeline, a map path and a written summary. (#95)
- The training library groups resources by kind and purpose. (#95)
- Manage your clan from the client. (#100)
- Browse a community calendar combining FAF events, tournaments and scheduled Discord events. (#105)
- Submit community events from the calendar. (#105)
- Browse the external-link directory. (#105)

## Playing, maps and replays

- Browse local replays without signing in and sign in from the offline workspace. (#95)
- Open game folders from a button in the sidebar. (#105)
- Replay launches reproduce generated maps before starting playback. (#69)
- Map previews show the full map name and provide a copy button. (#86)
- Generated-map previews show the generator settings. (#86)
- Keeping generated maps is controlled by one persistent setting. (#86)
- Replay details distinguish damaged recordings from recordings that ended early. (#95)
- Co-op replays display their recorded mission name. (#95)

## Mods and client behaviour

- Search mods by words in their name, description or UID, or choose an exact-name search. (#100)
- Installed mods show whether they are enabled for the next game. (#100)
- Mod descriptions provide usable links and a copy action. (#100)
- Publish a renamed copy of a mod without changing its original local folder. (#100)
- The window remembers its monitor, position, size and maximised state. (#86)
- Stable releases require installation before continuing when the client can install them. (#86)
- Desktop notifications follow the notification settings instead of mirroring every notice. (#86)
- A searched player's leaderboard rank is measured against the complete board. (#95)
- Retail Forged Alliance installations can be located through the reference clients' configuration. (#67)
