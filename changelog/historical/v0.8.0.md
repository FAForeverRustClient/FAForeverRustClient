Changes since v0.7.0.

## Tournaments

- Tournament creation includes format presets. (#375)
- Swiss formats include playoff cuts and opponent picking. (#375)
- Organisers can arrange the first Swiss round manually. (#375)
- Free-for-all team events support premade team names. (#375)
- Manage map pools and faction-veto rules from the tournament window. (#375)
- Tournament details have separate Matches, Vetoes and Maps tabs. (#373)
- Players can submit their own match scores. (#371)
- Organiser result edits retain replay IDs and correctly submit forfeits and declared winners. (#371)
- Configure third-place matches and best-of lengths by round or match. (#373)
- Manage entrants and teams using the organiser tools. (#375)
- Tournament overview shows schedules, recent results and import information. (#375)
- Imported tournaments can display group tables, final placings and a Stats tab. (#375)
- Use player views, streamer mode and their keyboard shortcuts. (#375)
- Pin tournament chat beside the other sections. (#375)
- Paste pictures into tournament text. (#375)
- Request editor or importer access from the client. (#375)

## Playing, maps and replays

- Play offline starts the installed FAF game from the login screen. (#405)
- Optionally show a running game on Steam, on Windows and Linux. (#366)
- Reorder columns in list views using the shared column controls. (#419)
- Fold away the custom-game details panel. (#398)
- Host-map filters and vault searches retain their values when revisiting them. (#400)
- Favourite maps from a game's large map preview. (#400)
- Map-pool previews open large, with a dedicated veto mode. (#405)
- A map veto is sent immediately when it is changed. (#405)
- Live replay lists can hide unranked games. (#365)
- Games still in progress are excluded from the online replay archive. (#405)
- Opening an online replay no longer creates an extra local copy. (#410)
- Generating many maps at once no longer times out after three minutes. (#410)
- A missing favourited map is downloaded before use. (#400)

## Reliability and notifications

- Your settings are no longer reset to the defaults when the client saves right after starting. (#398)
- Vault searches typed quickly no longer show the results of an older search. (#398)
- A message in the conversation you have open no longer shows a notification. (#400)
- Repeated notifications have a cooldown, and a full lobby is announced once. (#400)
- Get a notification when a matchmaking queue's map pool changes. (#400)
- Reviews include every version's reviews, ordered newest first. (#365)
- Player search finds offline players from the first few letters of their name. (#405)
- At interface scales above 100%, map previews and lobby popovers are no longer misplaced. (#410)
- The client's WebView2 processes always appear under the client in Task Manager. (#413)
- Update checks offer pre-releases by default. (#423)
- Russian localisation received additional corrections. (#376)
