Changes since v0.5.2.

## Playing and matchmaking

- Choose game, replay, map and mod folders in Settings, Paths. (#38)
- Choose whether generated maps are kept after use. (#38)
- See how many queued players could match your rating. (#40)
- Use the redesigned matchmaking cards and open party chat from the party panel. (#52)
- Browse map pools as map cards and adjust vetoes from the pool. (#53)
- Invite the same player again after an earlier party invitation. (#48)

## Replays, chat and vaults

- Find replays that two players played together. (#43)
- Replay player searches match the exact name by default. (#44)
- Vault sorting now follows the selected sort order. (#49)
- Place foes at the bottom of the chat roster. (#42)
- Large chat rooms and vault lists update faster, and map previews use a bounded amount of memory. (#54)

## Fixes

- The packaged client opens its own window instead of handing it to the browser. (#55)
- Joining downloads the host's map before requesting entry to the lobby. (#37)
- Arrow keys move selection through the host dialog's lists. (#37)
- Co-op hosting retains its draft when switching tabs. (#39)
- Selecting text and releasing outside a dialog no longer closes it. (#37)
- Account links open the FAF account service. (#37)
- Tooltips left behind by switching windows are dismissed. (#44)
